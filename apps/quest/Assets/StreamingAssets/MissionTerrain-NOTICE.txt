Beacon 0.21 Jezero VR scenery

Terrain and colour sources: NASA/JPL/University of Arizona/USGS.
https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/mars2020_trn/HiRISE/JEZ_hirise_soc_006_DTM_MOLAtopography_DeltaGeoid_1m_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_blend40.tif
https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/mars2020_trn/HiRISE/JEZ_hirise_soc_006_orthoMosaic_25cm_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_first.tif
https://asc-pds-services.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/mosaic/mars2020_trn/HiRISE/JEZ_hirise_soc_007_orthoMosaic_25cm_Ortho_blend120.tif

Beacon derives a 599 m vertex-span detail mesh, a 7 km distant mesh and a
static visual shadow mask from these sources. Geometry remains aligned to
the mission's exported coordinates. The shader blends generated sand and
layered-rock textures; small stones are illustrative procedural scenery.
Generated appearance is not new scientific data and does not alter the
engine's route, shelter selection, skyline, dose or sunlight calculations.

Full provenance, generation prompts and conversion instructions are in
Assets/Beacon/Art/JezeroTerrain/README.md, MarsLayeredRock.md and
MarsCosmeticDetail.md in the source repository.

The simple base habitat, solar racks and antenna are illustrative scenery,
anchored to the exported mission base. They do not change shelter calculations.
Arrival photos are bundled offline, with full provenance in
Assets/Resources/WaypointPhotos/README.md. PIA25681: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS;
PIA24921: NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS (enhanced colour, regional panorama).
Other named arrivals use the measured HiRISE image with a location marker.
