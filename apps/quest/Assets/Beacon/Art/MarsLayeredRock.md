# Illustrative layered sandstone

`MarsLayeredRock.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on 14 November 2026. It is an artistic surface material, not a recovered NASA photo, elevation measurement or geological analysis. It supplies fine colour and bump shading on the measured terrain; the original height grid and planner data are unchanged.

The generation prompt was:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square albedo texture for the vertical sandstone faces in a native Mars VR demo. Primary request: exceptionally crisp, believable close-up weathered Martian sedimentary sandstone, inspired by the thin layered rocks at Murray Buttes. Orthographic straight-on material scan covering approximately two metres square, no perspective, flat diffuse illumination with no directional cast shadows. Muted warm grey-brown, pale dusty ochre and restrained rusty tan; thin irregular horizontal beds, chipped flaky edges, subtle cracks, granular rock detail. Natural fine detail, moderate contrast, not saturated orange. All four edges tile seamlessly. No sky, horizon, standalone mountain, scene, objects, labels, text or watermark. Fill the entire image with the rock surface. This is illustrative game material, not scientific imagery. Output one square texture.

The native scene uses this material on steep surfaces and `MarsCosmeticDetail.png` on flatter ground. Both are explicitly credited as illustrative detail. The original Access Mars model remains archived in the project but is no longer the active walking surface.
